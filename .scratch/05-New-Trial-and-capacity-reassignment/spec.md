## Decision: capacity reassignment, not a rebuild

See `CONTEXT.md` → **Capacity reassignment**. The tenant, region (UK South) and workspaces stay
the same. Reassignment keeps workspace and item physical IDs, OneLake data, TDS endpoints, Git
bindings, the deployment pipeline and its rules, `vl_NYC_Crashes` active value sets, and
connections. The only thing that changes is the capacity ID.

Rejected: rebuilding from ADO. It would mean new physical IDs everywhere, recreating the
deployment pipeline and rules, re-consenting connections, a full re-CDC (~2.9 GB) and three stage
loads, and there are only three days.
