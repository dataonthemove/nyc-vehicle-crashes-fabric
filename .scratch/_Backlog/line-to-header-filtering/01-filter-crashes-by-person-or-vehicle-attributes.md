# Filter crash-level measures by person or vehicle attributes

Status: needs-triage
Category: enhancement
Raised: 2026-09-25 (header/line grilling)

> *This was generated by AI during the header/line grilling session and is a handover for later work.*

## Idea

Let a filter on a **line fact** attribute (e.g. `dim_person[person_type] = "Pedestrian"`,
`dim_vehicle[vehicle_type]`) restrict **header fact** measures on `fact_crashes`, e.g. "crashes
involving a pedestrian over 65".

## Why deferred

Out of scope for the header/line fix, which only pushes header keys down to the lines. The
reverse direction is an existence test, not a join: a crash has many persons, so no single
`person_key` fits on `fact_crashes`. It needs business definitions before any design:

- Do crash measures under the filter count all injuries in matching crashes, or only the matching persons'?
- Does selecting two values mean crashes with *either* or with *both*?

## Candidate approaches

- Crash-level summary attributes on the header (an involvement-profile junk dimension, e.g.
  *has pedestrian*, *has cyclist*, *oldest person band*) — Kimball's usual answer.
- A defined many-to-many path through `collision_id`, with semantics fixed in measures.
  Beware ambiguous paths via `dim_date` and the ~1M-row DirectQuery fallback on large key sets.
